Researcher in Mathematics
and Statistical Analysis
of Financial Markets

I develop theoretical models and analytical frameworks for the study of financial markets, grounded in mathematical and statistical principles, with a specific focus on approaches independent of technical indicators and oscillators.

My work is centered on the construction of non-optimized analytical structures aimed at describing market behavior in a rigorous, coherent, and reproducible way.

Research Areas

01 Mathematical analysis of financial markets
02 Statistical modeling of price dynamics
03 Development of indicator-free trading frameworks
04 Critical analysis of traditional technical analysis
05 Formulation of alternative theoretical approaches to market interpretation
