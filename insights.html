Study I

Theory of Temporal Arcs

Developed through mathematical and statistical analysis of temporal structures, focusing on order book dynamics and volume interactions in futures markets. Originating in 1997 from DAX futures volume sequences, the study constructs a real-time directional framework extended across daily, weekly, monthly, and annual cycles. It represents an alternative framework for quantitative market analysis, tested on major indices, Forex, and commodities.

Academic Context

Published by the Shanghai Advanced Institute of Finance in 2010 and connected to the Master’s Degree Program in Financial Market Derivatives.

Study II

AC STOP

An innovative daily stop-loss and Stop & Reverse methodology designed for predefined application across tradable financial instruments. Built on recurring structural configurations, the framework identifies the technical thresholds of trend deterioration to optimize risk control and reduce drawdown exposure under high volatility stress.

Academic Context

Published in 2011 by Waseda University, Tokyo, within the Master’s Program in Technical Analysis Applied to Financial Markets.

Clarity

Relationship with Academic Use

The Insights section may contain full studies, analytical texts, and methodological notes.

The Academic Use section is reserved for studies or materials that have been considered, published, or referenced within master-level university contexts.

When a study belongs to both areas, it may appear in Insights as a full analytical contribution and in Academic Use as an academic reference. This distinction allows the website to separate the analytical value of a study from its academic consideration, while preserving a coherent connection between the two sections.
